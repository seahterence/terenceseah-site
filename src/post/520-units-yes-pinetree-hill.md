## Introduction

Pinetree Hill, jointly developed by UOL and Singapore Land, boasts 88% landscape footprint to lift the living experience another level. Large communal spaces are definitely welcome to home owners — especially after our pandemic lock-down experience in 2020.

Consisting of only three towers, staggered in position, every unit gets unobstructed views while still enjoying the large communal spaces and facilities. UOL has always been renowned for lush landscaping that home owners truly enjoy, even more so in a concrete jungle like Singapore.

## Locale

Situated in District 21, within the prestigious Mount Sinai and Pandan Valley neighbourhood, with top-tier educational institutions, commercial, dining and lifestyle options nearby. Nature adventure awaits along the Rail Corridor, the Ulu Pandan Park Connector, the hidden gem of Clementi Forest, the upcoming Old Jurong Line and the Clementi Nature Trail.

> I enjoy this location — serene and convenient for my cycling and photography, and peaceful at nightfall.

![The surroundings at Pinetree Hill](/img/pt-locale.jpg)

## Facilities

There are 42 facilities catered to residents, which is very generous — but whether I would eventually use them all is another question. As with most developments these days, facilities are like buying a car with the latest equipment: heads-up display, GPS, AirPlay. From my observations, home hunters definitely dwell on this aspect, and the more the merrier.

I appreciate the developer's dedication to thoughtfully drawing out all the various communal spaces without intruding on the privacy of the homes, thanks to the high elevation of the units.

![Facilities at Pinetree Hill](/img/pt-facil.jpg)

## Finishing

In this modern era it is ever more competitive on the fittings that come with a development, and Pinetree Hill does not disappoint. Bosch, De Dietrich and Samsung are some of the brands you will find here. It serves its function and is aesthetically pleasing to me, but the brands give me assurance on reliability. Carpentry work is decent and efficiently designed enough for me to feel a good sense of getting what I pay for.

## Layout

There is variety from one-plus-study to five-bedroom units, and I must admit it is very well designed and space efficient — meaning the usable areas are well thought out and catered to how people actually live. Due to stringent requirements in Singapore, layout designs are similar most of the time; the only differences are minor variations in the size of spaces such as living, dining, bedroom and washroom.

Let us agree on one thing: every household has its own requirements, and there will never be a perfect size or home unless we build it ourselves. The perception of space differs from person to person — but for me, I do enjoy the space I experienced throughout the unit types.
